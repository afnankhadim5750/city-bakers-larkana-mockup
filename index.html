No. 02 — The House Fame

The plain cake, first.

Ask Larkana about City Bakers and the answer comes back in one word: plain cake. Reviewers name it again and again — not the frosted showpiece, not the newest flavour, but the plain sponge, golden outside and soft inside, bought by the whole cake and eaten at home.

It is the kind of cake that travels. One reviewer bought five to take to Dubai as gifts — a plain cake, carried across a border, because it tastes like home.
